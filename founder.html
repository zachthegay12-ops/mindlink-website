Meet The Founder

Zachary Chase Milutin

Founder & Creator of MindLink

Why I Created MindLink

MindLink was created with the goal of helping people find support, resources, and a place where they feel understood. Mental health affects many people, but finding the right information and support can sometimes feel overwhelming. MindLink was built to help make those resources easier to discover and create a community focused on kindness, awareness, and connection.

What I Believe

Everyone Deserves Support

People should have access to resources and information when they need it.

Small Steps Matter

Progress does not happen overnight. Every step toward wellness matters.

Community Helps

Connection and kindness can make a difference.

Thank You For Being Here

MindLink is only the beginning. The goal is to continue growing, creating resources, and helping more people.
