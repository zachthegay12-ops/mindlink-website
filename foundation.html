Building A Better Future

MindLink is working toward becoming a mental health wellness foundation focused on education, support, and community connection.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make mental health resources easier to access while creating a welcoming environment where people feel supported, heard, and connected.

Our Goals

Education

Create mental health guides, resources, and learning materials.

Community

Build safe spaces where people can connect and support each other.

Future Hotline

Work toward developing a future support hotline and assistance network.

Outreach

Partner with communities and organizations to spread awareness.

Future Projects

MindLink hopes to expand into more mental health programs, support tools, community events, and future services that help people find care.
